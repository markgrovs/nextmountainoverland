A Cairn is a reusable learning or review. It can be linked to the trip where the lesson arose and found independently later. This sample contains no equipment recommendation.

## The question

Describe the problem being solved and the circumstances in which the advice applies.

## What was tried

Record the approach, relevant observations, and limitations. A real review would distinguish personal experience from information that still needs verification.

## What to carry forward

Summarize the useful lesson in the author's words. The contents navigation helps readers return to a section, and the last-verified date provides context when advice gets older.
