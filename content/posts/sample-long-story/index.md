> **Design sample — not an actual travel account.** Dates, places, and media demonstrate the layout.

A longer narrative belongs in the same Journal as short daily notes. This sample demonstrates headings and paragraphs rather than inventing an experience for the travelers.

<!--more-->

## Setting out

An opening can establish the place and the question behind a day. The author might describe what drew their attention, what they expected, or what changed. It does not need to become an itinerary.

## A moment worth remembering

This is room for a fuller scene: a conversation, an unexpected stop, or the small details that are easy to forget. Photographs can appear between passages, with their own captions and natural proportions.

## Looking back

A closing reflection can bring the story together. Length does not change the entry's home: it remains in the Journal and in its associated Journey.
